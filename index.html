15. With A Miracle

By John Edic Walton

Once upon a time there lived a man Warred Belifit. His name signifies he can believe even when there's tragedy. He believed he can live and measure his life when his treason friend named Philadelphia wanted to make an elixir with combination of Hazardous substances and put it on the wine so that it makes invisible when Belifit drinks it...

When Belifit was offered the wine, he first smelled it. Recognizing it was dangerous and poisonous, he rejected the disloyal trap. Though their companionship broke and Belifit was left alone, he didn't complain. Guided by his faith—"With A Miracle"—he sets out to succeed independently and expose Philadelphia's treason!

Moral Lesson: Even if you taste, eat and smell, you must also search if it is poisonous/deadly so that "With A Miracle" can save you from death!
